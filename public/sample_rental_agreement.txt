RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on January 10, 2026, by and between Bayview Apartments LLC ("Landlord") and Jane Smith ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND TERM
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the real property located at 456 Ocean View Drive, Apt 3B, San Francisco, CA 94107 ("Premises"). The lease term shall begin on February 1, 2026, and expire on January 31, 2027.

2. RENT AND SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly rent in the amount of $2,800 due on the first day of each calendar month. Upon signing, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $2,800 as a Security Deposit. The Security Deposit shall be refunded within 21 days following lease termination, minus lawful deductions for unpaid rent or damages.

3. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Landlord shall maintain the structural components, plumbing, heating, and electrical systems in good repair. Tenant agrees to keep the Premises clean, report maintenance issues within 48 hours, and refrain from making structural alterations without prior written consent.

4. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL OR TERMINATION
Tenant or Landlord must provide at least 60 days written notice prior to the lease expiration date if either party intends not to renew this Lease.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by and construed under the laws of the State of California and local municipal codes of the City and County of San Francisco.

Landlord: Bayview Apartments LLC
Tenant: Jane Smith
